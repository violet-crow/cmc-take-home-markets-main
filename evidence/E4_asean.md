# E4: ASEAN: the Variation Guideline and its members

Primary-source notes collected by the regulatory team. Each note says what the cited source establishes and nothing more. Where the team did not find a source, the note says so.

## The ASEAN Variation Guideline

The ASEAN Variation Guideline (AVG), Revision 2, is published by the ASEAN Consultative Committee for Standards and Quality: Pharmaceutical Product Working Group (ACCSQ-PPWG). It defines three variation categories: **MaV** (major variation, prior approval), **MiV-PA** (minor variation, prior approval) and **MiV-N** (minor variation, notification). Nobody files with ACCSQ-PPWG: every marketing authorisation in the region is granted, and every variation filed, with a national authority.

ASEAN has eleven member states: Brunei Darussalam, Cambodia, Indonesia, Lao PDR, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Timor-Leste and Viet Nam.

## Per member

| Member | Authority | What the reviewed source establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Brunei Darussalam | Brunei Darussalam Medicines Control Authority (BDMCA) | BDMCA publishes MaV/MiV submission procedures. |
| Cambodia | Department of Drugs and Food (DDF), Ministry of Health | The [registration guide of 1 August 2019](https://ddf.moh.gov.kh/public/1637580331_Therapeutic%20Product%20in%20Cambodia_01%20August%202019.pdf) contains a variation guideline (pp. 125–127). The reviewed materials do not establish whether it takes its categories from the AVG. |
| Indonesia | BPOM | A national baseline under [PerBPOM 24/2017](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/220387/perka-bpom-no-24-tahun-2017), amended by PerBPOM 15/2023; [PerBPOM 23/2025](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/328077/peraturan-bpom-no-23-tahun-2025), Article 1, retains the national Major, Minor and Notifikasi definitions. |
| Lao PDR | Food and Drug Department, Ministry of Health | The reviewed materials do not establish a national variation guideline or its relationship to the AVG. |
| Malaysia | NPRA | The [Malaysian Variation Guideline, 2nd Edition, July 2022](https://www.npra.gov.my/easyarticles/images/users/1131/MVG-for-Pharmaceutical-Products-2nd-Edition-July-2022.pdf), introduction, identifies AVG Revision 2 as its basis and includes national requirements. |
| Myanmar | Department of Food and Drug Administration, Ministry of Health | The reviewed materials do not establish a national variation guideline or its relationship to the AVG. |
| Philippines | FDA Philippines | [FDA Circular 2014-008](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/79273), section B, places country-specific requirements alongside the ASEAN categories. The [Philippine Variation Guidelines V1.0, 22 April 2020](https://www.fda.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Philippine-Variation-Guidelines-V.1.0-with-fees-and-charges.pdf) print national `MaV-PH`, `MiV-PH` and `MiV-PH-N` families alongside AVG-numbered entries. |
| Singapore | Health Sciences Authority (HSA) | HSA publishes its own categories under its own labels: see the table below. |
| Thailand | Thai FDA | The Thai FDA notification of 28 September 2023 and its [guidance page](https://drug.fda.moph.go.th/announcement-fda) identify the AVG as a source, alongside other frameworks. |
| Timor-Leste | National Directorate of Pharmacy and Medicines (DNFM), Ministry of Health | The reviewed materials do not establish a national variation guideline or its relationship to the AVG. |
| Viet Nam | Drug Administration of Vietnam | Circular 12/2025/TT-BYT, Appendix II, uses a national major/minor variation scheme. |

## Singapore's published categories

From HSA's *Guidance on Therapeutic Product Registration in Singapore*, Appendices 13–14.

| Code | Name | Route |
|---|---|---|
| MAV-1 | Major variation application | Prior approval |
| MAV-2 | Major variation application: forensic reclassification | Prior approval |
| MIV-1 | Minor variation requiring prior approval | Prior approval |
| MIV-2-NOTIFICATION | Minor variation notification (40-working-day standstill) | Notify, then wait |
| MIV-2-DO-AND-TELL | Minor variation, do and tell (submit within 6 months) | Notify at implementation |
| NO-NOTIFICATION | Change not requiring notification to HSA | No filing |
